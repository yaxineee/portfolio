Process

The real workflow

No committees. No 30-slide briefs. One WhatsApp thread, one site shipped in the period we agreed.

  • 01

    Scope the work

    We agree on what the site needs to do, who it is for, and what success looks like. No 40-slide brief. Just one focused conversation.

  • 02

    Reserve the slot

    A deposit holds your spot in the queue. We start building once it is in.

  • 03

    Build in the period we agreed

    Design, build, and content. I work in the timeframe we set up front. No surprise extensions, no scope creep.

  • 04

    You review and give feedback

    When the first version is ready, you take a real look. Send notes, not just a thumbs up. The site is shaped by what actually serves your visitors.

  • 05

    Refinements

    One focused round of revisions. We go through your notes together and the site gets sharper.

  • 06

    Launch

    You approve, pay the balance, and the site goes live. Clean handoff: domain, hosting, access, walkthrough.

  • 07

    period of technical support guaranteed

    Any bug. Any hosting glitch. Any small change you forgot. We fix it for a full year after delivery, no extra charge.
